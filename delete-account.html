Delete my account

This page lets any customer of the Mahakal Wellness app delete his or her account. You do not need the app installed.

What is deleted

  • your login and password, and every phone where you were logged in
  • your profile photo, date of birth and WhatsApp number
  • your registration forms, password-reset requests, and the reminders and birthday wishes shown in the app

What we keep

  • your orders, bills, payments and wallet records, because accounting and tax law require us to keep them
  • your hall bookings and their advance payment details, because they are our business records
  • the messages you sent us through the contact form. You can ask our team to remove a message (see the end of this page)
  • your name, mobile number, DS ID, city and upline on our customer list. You can ask our team to remove this too (see the end of this page); we will do it unless the law requires us to keep it or you still have a wallet balance

Deleting cannot be undone. You can register again later, and our team will approve your new account.
