Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026  ·  Applies to the Mahakal Wellness website and Android app

In short

  • We collect only what we need to take your order, keep your account and send your bill.
  • We do not sell your information and we show no advertisements.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time: in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or on the delete-account page.
  • Short me: aapki details sirf order, bill aur account ke liye hain. Bechte nahi hain. Account aap khud delete kar sakte ho.

1. Who we are

Mahakal Wellness (“we”, “us”) distributes wellness products from 369 Ashoka Vihar, Caps Tower, Pandri, Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India. This policy explains what personal information our website and app collect, why we collect it, who sees it and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

When you register an account: your mobile number, name, DS ID, city, WhatsApp number, your upline’s name and (if you choose to give it) your upline’s mobile number, your date of birth (optional), a password and a profile photo (optional). Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it.

When you place an order: your name, mobile number, city, customer type, ID name, DS ID, reference (upline) name and mobile number, the products and amounts you order, and the payment details you give us: payment method, UTR / reference number, amount, date and time.

Payment screenshot (optional): if you upload a screenshot of your payment, the picture is read automatically to fill in the amount, UTR and date for you (see “Who we share information with”), and is then kept in private storage so that our team can check your payment.

Notifications (optional): if you allow notifications, your phone or browser gives us a notification address so that we can send you updates. It is not linked to your name or account. You can switch notifications off at any time in your phone or browser settings.

Technical information: like most online services, our hosting providers may log technical details such as your IP address and the type of device or browser, to keep the service secure and running. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

If you give us the name or mobile number of another person (for example your upline), please make sure they are fine with it.

3. How we use it

  • to create and check your account, and to log you in;
  • to take, bill, deliver and track your orders, and to show your bills, dues, offers and progress;
  • to check payments and to catch wrong or duplicate payment entries;
  • to send your bill, receipts, payment reminders and updates by WhatsApp or app notification, and to wish you on your birthday if you gave your date of birth;
  • to credit the purchase points (SP) of your order with the company whose products we distribute;
  • to keep accounting and tax records, and to answer your questions.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only as needed to run the service:

  • Service providers who run the app for us: Supabase (database, file storage and server functions) and our website hosting providers (such as Amazon Web Services).
  • Payment screenshot reading: when you upload a payment screenshot, the image is sent to our service provider Anthropic (an AI service) only to read the amount, UTR and date, and the result comes back to us.
  • The company whose products we distribute: your DS ID, name and order details, to credit the purchase points (SP) of your purchase.
  • WhatsApp: when our team messages you on WhatsApp, the message travels through WhatsApp under its own terms.
  • Google: our pages load their text fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.
  • Authorities: when the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account information (login, photo, date of birth, WhatsApp number): until you delete your account.
  • Orders, bills, payments, wallet records and payment screenshots: as long as accounting and tax law, and the settling of any dispute, require. We keep these even after your account is deleted, because they are our business records.
  • Notification addresses: until you switch notifications off or the address stops working.
  • When our team removes a customer-list entry, a private backup copy of that entry is kept so that mistakes can be undone.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open Profile → Delete my account, or use the delete-account page. This removes your login and password, saved logins, profile photo, date of birth, WhatsApp number, registration forms, password-reset requests, and the reminders and birthday wishes shown in the app. It does not remove your name, mobile number, DS ID, city and upline from our customer list, or your orders, bills, payments and wallet records.
  • Remove your customer-list entry too: ask our team (contact below). We will do it unless the law requires us to keep it or you still have a wallet balance.
  • Correct your details: tell our team and we will update them.
  • Notifications: switch them off in your phone or browser settings.

7. Security

Our pages and servers use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Profile photos and payment screenshots are kept in private storage that only our logged-in staff can open. No online service can promise perfect security, so please choose a password that others cannot guess.

8. Children

Our service is for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
